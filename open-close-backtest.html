Open Close Backtest

Tests whether buying at the open and selling at the close, or shorting at the open and buying back at the close, can outperform buy-and-hold. It replays five years of daily prices, compounding a $100,000 balance after the selected round-trip cost. Intraday positions close each day; dividends and stock-borrow fees are excluded.
