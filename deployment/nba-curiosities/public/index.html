What exactly is being averaged?

Per-game averages are total statistics divided by appearances. In at least one season / run mode, all games within the selected age bracket are combined within each season. In all games in age bracket mode, they are combined across all selected seasons, weighted by games played. The two modes can produce different answers.

Age is calculated on each game date. A season crossing an age-bracket boundary is split. Career overlaps can be satisfied in different seasons. Same-season stories require every condition in at least one shared season; their evidence shows those seasons explicitly.

How discoveries are found

The server discovers thresholds from the observed distributions of every supported statistic, tests overlaps, removes redundant combinations, and verifies every published result. It searches all history and a rotating decade each day. The source remains the fixed 1950–2025 snapshot; discovery is automatic, new data acquisition is not. One-game achievements remain eligible.
