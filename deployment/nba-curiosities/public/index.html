These are descriptive facts about a historical snapshot. Uniqueness is a property of the filters and available records, not a prediction or a statistical significance test.

What exactly is being averaged?

Per-game averages are total statistics divided by appearances. In at least one season / run mode, all games within the selected age bracket are combined within each season. In all games in age bracket mode, they are combined across all selected seasons, weighted by games played. The two modes can produce different answers.

Age is calculated on each game date. A season crossing an age-bracket boundary is split. Different conditions can be satisfied in different seasons; the overlap describes career memberships.

How discoveries are found

We enumerate round-number thresholds across points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks and three-pointers, plus combinations of age brackets and statistics. Findings with the same players and condition family are deduplicated. A one-game achievement is retained and its sample size shown.
